# Astroturf Missing Info Checklist

Do not finalize pricing or a customer-ready scope until the following are confirmed.

## Measurements

- exact length and width of each turf strip/section
- total square footage
- number and location of corners, cutouts, and obstacles
- elevation differences / slope conditions

## Product decisions

- turf brand/product
- pile height / face weight / quality level
- whether infill is required and which type
- whether weed barrier is desired
- intended use: decorative, pet, light traffic, etc.

## Edge/details

- edge treatment method at concrete
- edge treatment method at house/foundation side
- whether nails/spikes/adhesive/edging are preferred or required
- seam locations, if any

## Site conditions

- current soil condition and depth of unsuitable material
- presence of sprinklers, drip lines, valves, conduit, or utilities
- disposal plan for removed sod/soil/debris
- access constraints for hauling material and using a compactor
- whether drainage corrections beyond minor grading are needed

## Customer/business details

- client budget target
- whether materials are owner-supplied or contractor-supplied
- whether haul-off is included
- schedule/timing expectations

## Red-flag unknowns

- hidden roots
- buried utilities or irrigation
- soft pockets/subsidence
- foundation-adjacent drainage issues
