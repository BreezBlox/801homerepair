## Estimate structure defaults

For customer-facing estimates:
- separate labor from materials
- include prep work where applicable
- include one-time fees when they are real and defensible
- include assumptions and exclusions
- avoid exploding the estimate into dozens of tiny line items unless the user asks for that

## Labor presentation preference

The user prefers labor to feel justified and professional rather than appearing as one raw lump with no explanation.

Good framing:
- labor includes installation time, setup, standard tool use, basic planning, and normal jobsite coordination where applicable
- fees can be grouped where that keeps the estimate readable
- do not hide unusual prep, demolition, disposal, or transition work inside a vague labor blob

## Materials rules

Always clarify one of these:
- customer pickup
- contractor pickup
- contractor supply and markup
- still undecided

If customer pickup:
- verify exact store location and live availability before telling the customer where to go
- list exact product names, quantities, and any matching accessories needed

## Quoting discipline

Before finalizing a number, confirm:
1. quantities and units
2. which prep items actually apply
3. transitions / openings count
4. disposal responsibility
5. whether baseboard / trim is included
6. whether floor leveling is assumed, confirmed, or excluded
7. who is handling materials
8. whether trip/setup/consumables should be shown separately or folded into the labor presentation

## Scope wording guidance

Use wording like:
- "Estimate assumes standard install conditions unless hidden damage or additional prep is found."
- "Materials are excluded unless specifically listed."
- "Floor leveling, repair of hidden subfloor damage, and other unforeseen conditions are not included unless noted."
- "Pricing is based on the measurements and scope currently provided."

## Example calculation pattern

Example flooring estimate logic:
- plank install = sqft × $2.00
- carpet removal = sqft × $0.50 if applicable
- subfloor prep = sqft × $0.15 if applicable
- floor leveling = sqft × $0.50 if applicable
- trim install = linft × listed rate if applicable
- transitions / openings = count × $25
- add one-time fees that truly apply

Then separate:
- labor / prep total
- materials total
- grand total

Do not pretend flooring-specific rates automatically apply to unrelated handyman work.
