FAQ

Common questions

Do you do small jobs?

Yes. Small-to-mid projects are the focus, including punch-list work and single-room repairs.

How fast can you come out?

Response is typically fast, based on current schedule and project scope. Reach out with your city/zip and details for availability.

Do you haul away old flooring?

Yes. Carpet, pad, and tack strip removal plus haul-away can be included in your quote.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes. Tenant turnover scope is a core service, including doors, flooring, touchups, and punch-list items.

How do estimates work?

Share your scope, photos, dimensions, and location. You will get a labor estimate and, when needed, an on-site walkthrough for final pricing.

What do you need to quote accurately?

City/zip, job type, rough dimensions, target timeframe, and photos of the current condition.
