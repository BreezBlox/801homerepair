Phone Numbers

If you provide a phone number, it may be used to call or text you directly about your project, appointment, estimate, or service visit.

Phone numbers are not shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Service Providers

We may use service providers such as website hosting, form handling, calendar, email, payment, or messaging tools to operate the business. These providers are used only as needed to provide services and run business operations.

Retention

Basic project and appointment records may be kept for scheduling, estimating, payment, warranty, tax, or business record purposes. You can request correction or deletion of your information by contacting us, subject to any records we need to keep for legitimate business or legal reasons.
