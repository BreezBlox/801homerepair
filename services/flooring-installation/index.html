Common questions

Before you request a flooring quote

These answers help us spot the details that can change the job plan.

Do you install LVP and vinyl plank flooring?

Yes, when the project fits the available scope and site conditions. Send photos, rough dimensions, and your material details first.

Do you handle tear-out or floor preparation?

Sometimes. Existing flooring removal, leveling, subfloor repairs, disposal, and unusual prep need to be reviewed before they are included.

Can I provide the flooring myself?

Yes, customer-supplied material can be reviewed. Include the product name, amount on hand, and any installation instructions when you request a quote.

Do you work on stairs, landings, and transitions?

Those projects can be reviewed from photos. Include close-ups of stair edges, thresholds, trim, and the areas where floors meet.

What should I send to get started?

Send photos, approximate measurements, the job city or address area, your timing, and whether you already have the flooring.

Tell us what you need updated.

Photos and a clear description help catch prep, material, access, and transition questions before the visit.
