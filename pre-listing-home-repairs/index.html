Repair before replacing

Before you replace it, let’s see if it can be fixed.

The goal is to present the home well without over-improving it. I’ll review the condition and tell you what looks practical.

Repair

Damaged baseboard

Repair or replace the affected section when the surrounding trim is sound.

Assess

A few bad floor planks

Check whether a localized repair is practical for the existing floor.

Refresh

Scuffed or damaged wall

Patch and touch up paint where the finish can be matched acceptably.

Consider

Tired-looking cabinets

See whether touch-up or repainting makes sense before replacement.

Clean

Dirty concrete or siding

Pressure washing can refresh an appropriate surface before listing.

Every home and material is different. I’ll confirm the scope after reviewing the details; hidden conditions or added work may change the estimate.
